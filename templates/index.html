DeskWell AI

Your Personal Desk Wellness Coach.

Long hours at a desk can lead to back, neck and eye strain, often because we don't notice our posture or forget to take breaks. DeskWell AI helps you become aware of how you sit, take regular movement breaks and review your daily activity.

Key Features

Posture Awareness

Webcam-based posture estimates using simple rules, processed on your own computer.

Break Reminders

Timely nudges to stand up, stretch and rest your eyes.

Stretch Guide

Simple desk-friendly stretches with clear instructions.

Daily Summary

Review your sessions, breaks and posture warnings.

Supporting the Sustainable Development Goals

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being

Encourages posture awareness, regular breaks and healthier desk habits. It is a wellness aid, not a medical tool.

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

Free and open-source, using only a normal webcam and browser, with no paid services or special equipment, so it is easier for more people to access.

Our Privacy Promise

Your webcam video is processed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded or saved. Only simple session summaries (like duration and break counts) are stored on your own computer.
